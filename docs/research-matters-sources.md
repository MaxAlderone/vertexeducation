## Admissions copy

- **Harvard:** The [2020 appeals court opinion, pp. 14–15](https://www.harvard.edu/admissionscase/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2021/06/first_circuit_court_of_appeals_opinion.pdf#page=14) describes historical academic ratings and how the highest rating could recognize original scholarship. It does not establish an eightfold admissions advantage caused by research. The requested [2018 filing](https://mindingthecampus.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Doc-414-Statement-of-Material-Facts.pdf) remains linked as related reading, rather than the evidentiary basis for a multiplier claim.
- **Penn:** The [Class of 2026 announcement](https://almanac.upenn.edu/articles/admission-decisions-for-the-class-of-2026), published 12 April 2022 and signed by E. Whitney Soule, reports that nearly one-third of admitted students engaged in academic research in high school.
- **Caltech:** The [Class of 2027 announcement](https://www.caltech.edu/about/news/caltech-extends-admissions-offers-to-class-of-2027), published 27 March 2023, reports that 45% of admitted students included materials documenting past research and more than one-third submitted creative and maker portfolios.
- **Forbes:** [Christopher Rim’s 18 June 2024 article](https://www.forbes.com/sites/christopherrim/2024/06/18/the-admissions-criteria-that-top-schools-really-care-about/) discusses academic rigor, extracurricular involvement, personal qualities, and recommendations. It is labeled as commentary; research-specific claims are not attributed to this article.

The statistics describe historical admitted classes. They do not isolate the effect of research on an individual applicant’s admission chances.
