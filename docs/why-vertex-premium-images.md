# Why Vertex Research premium images

Created with the built-in image generation tool (`image_gen`), replacing the homepage doodles with a coordinated series of editorial still-life images. All seven assets use research objects, with no people or human-like figures.

Selected assets are saved in `public/why-vertex/` as `*-premium.webp`. The generated images are resized to 1200 pixels wide and encoded as WebP at quality 85 for web delivery. Image content is not altered during conversion. The original doodles remain available as earlier versions.

## Art direction

Warm ivory limestone and paper, deep ink-green accents, satin brushed brass, soft directional window light, and restrained photographic detail. Each scene represents the benefit beside it. The mentorship image is the style reference for the remaining six images. University logos and admissions excerpts are unaffected.

## Mentorship prompt

Asset: `public/why-vertex/expert-mentorship-premium.webp`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one landscape editorial still-life illustration for the "Why Vertex Research" section of a premium research mentorship website. Output one image, 1536 x 1024, 3:2 landscape.
Primary request: communicate thoughtful one-to-one expert research mentorship entirely through objects.
Scene: a substantial open ivory reference book with a deep ink-green cloth cover, opposite a smaller open research notebook, arranged as a quiet exchange of ideas. A single elegant brushed-brass arc bridges the two books, with a small brass fountain pen beside the notebook. Pages carry only sparse abstract diagrams and fine unlettered lines. No graduation cap or speech bubbles.
Style: high-end editorial still-life, exquisitely art-directed sculptural 3D with photographic realism, refined physical materials and believable proportions. Warm ivory limestone tabletop and seamless soft ivory studio backdrop (#f4efe5), pale paper, dark ink green (#173d37), restrained satin gold/brass (#b18b4a), a tiny touch of smoked glass. Nothing glossy or toy-like.
Composition: softly elevated three-quarter view, unified central object group taking about 65 percent of the canvas, beautiful balanced negative space and generous safe margins on all sides. All objects fully visible, no cropping. A single scene, no collage, no panels, no frame.
Lighting: large soft window light from upper left, gentle realistic contact shadows, subtle material grain, calm intelligent academic atmosphere. Restrained contrast, no harsh spotlights.
Strict constraints: NO people, hands, faces, silhouettes, human-like figures, person icons, mascots, readable text, letters, labels, wordmarks, university logos or watermarks. No doodle outlines, no cartoon effects, no floating confetti or decorative stars.
```
