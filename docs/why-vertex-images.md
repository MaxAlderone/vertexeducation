# Why Vertex Research doodles

These are the earlier illustrations. The homepage now uses the [premium image set](why-vertex-premium-images.md).

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Every image uses objects and symbols to explain its benefit, with no people, faces, hands, silhouettes, or human-like characters. These doodles replace the earlier human illustrations on the homepage.

The seven selected doodles are saved under `public/why-vertex/` with `-doodle.webp` filenames. They are converted from generated PNGs to 1200-pixel-wide WebP files at quality 82 for web delivery. No scene edits are made during conversion. Next.js Image serves responsive versions, with `object-fit: contain` keeping the complete drawing visible.

## Final prompts and assets

No realistic rendering, no photography, no 3D, no detailed shadows or textures.

No readable words, captions, logos, university crests, or watermarks. Small abstract chart marks and a simple question mark are allowed.
```text
Scene and meaning: Doodle a large open reference book on the left and a smaller open research notebook on the right, linked by two curved arrows flowing both directions. Above the notebooks, two small overlapping empty speech bubbles suggest a personal exchange of ideas. A small academic mortarboard rests beside the reference book. Clearly communicate one-to-one academic guidance through objects only.
```
